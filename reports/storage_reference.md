
# AtmoSync - Agricultural Storage Reference

## Purpose
Document reliable product-specific storage recommendations
for agricultural shipment monitoring.

## 1. Apple (Golden Delicious reference)
- Optimum temperature: 0°C ± 1°C
- Relative humidity: 90-95%
- Note: This reference is specifically for Golden Delicious.
- Source: UC Davis Postharvest Research Center
- URL: https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/produce-facts-sheets/apple-golden-delicious

## 2. Avocado
- Mature-green: 5-13°C
- Ripe: 2-4°C
- Relative humidity: 90-95%
- Note: Temperature depends on cultivar and storage duration.
- Source: UC Davis Postharvest Research Center
- URL: https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/produce-facts-sheets/avocado

## 3. Mango
- Mature-green: 13°C
- Partially-ripe and ripe: 10°C
- Relative humidity: 90-95%
- Note: Temperature depends on maturity stage.
- Source: UC Davis Postharvest Research Center
- URL: https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/produce-facts-sheets/mango

## Important Considerations
- These are recommended storage conditions.
- They are not universal spoilage thresholds.
- Product variety and maturity must be considered.
- Exposure duration must be considered in future risk analysis.
- Further validation is required before assigning spoilage-risk labels.
